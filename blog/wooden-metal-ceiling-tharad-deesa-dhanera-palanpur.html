Wooden Metal Ceiling Project — Tharad, Deesa, Dhanera & Palanpur

Yashvi Infra Co has completed wooden finish metal ceiling installations across Tharad, Deesa, Dhanera and Palanpur in Banaskantha, Gujarat. The project brings the warm, natural look of timber together with the strength of a metal ceiling system, giving each space an elegant finish that lasts.

Project Overview

Tharad, Deesa, Dhanera and Palanpur are busy commercial and residential centres of Banaskantha district, with new showrooms, offices, clinics, hotels and homes coming up every year. Clients here want ceilings that look premium but can cope with hot summers, dust and humidity. For this project we installed wood-look metal ceiling panels that deliver the appearance of a wooden ceiling without the upkeep problems of real timber.

Why Choose a Wooden Finish Metal Ceiling?

  • Natural wood look: Woodgrain finishes on metal panels give the warmth and richness of a timber ceiling in a wide range of tones.
  • Termite and moisture resistance: Unlike real wood, metal panels do not rot, warp, swell or attract termites, which makes them a dependable choice in this region.
  • Long life, low maintenance: The finish holds its colour and can be wiped clean, so the ceiling keeps looking good with minimal care.
  • Fire safety: Metal is non-combustible, adding a layer of safety for commercial and public spaces.
  • Clean, quick installation: Pre-finished panels fit into a concealed or exposed grid system, so work is faster and cleaner than traditional carpentry.
  • Easy service access: Panels can be lifted out to reach wiring, lighting and air-conditioning ducts above the ceiling.

Where This Ceiling Works Best

Wooden metal ceilings suit a wide range of spaces across Tharad, Deesa, Dhanera and Palanpur, including shop and showroom interiors, offices and reception areas, restaurants and hotels, hospitals and clinics, schools and institutions, and the entrance lobbies and living areas of homes.

Materials & Specifications Used

The ceiling used pre-finished metal panels with a woodgrain surface, fixed on a galvanised T-grid suspension system chosen to suit the span and load of each space. Panel size, plank width and finish tone were matched to the interior design of each site, and edges and joints were aligned carefully so the grain flows evenly across the ceiling.

Installation Process

Execution across the Tharad, Deesa, Dhanera and Palanpur sites followed our standard process:

  1. Site survey, measurement and ceiling layout planning
  2. Selection of woodgrain finish and panel pattern with the client
  3. Suspension grid and perimeter channel installation
  4. Panel fixing with alignment and level checks for uniform spacing
  5. Cut-outs for lights, AC vents and sprinklers, followed by final quality inspection, cleanup and client handover

Result

The completed ceilings give each client the look of fine wood with the durability of metal — a practical, good-looking and low-maintenance upgrade for homes, shops, offices and commercial buildings across Tharad, Deesa, Dhanera, Palanpur and the wider Banaskantha district.
